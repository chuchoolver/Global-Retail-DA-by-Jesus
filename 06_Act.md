# 🚀 Act

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this phase was to translate the analysis findings into actionable business recommendations that can support better decision-making and improve sales and profitability.

--- 

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings identified during the analysis, the following actions could help improve business performance:

### 1. Review Discount Strategies

High discount levels were associated with lower profitability. Discount policies should therefore be reviewed to ensure that increased sales volume does not come at the expense of sustainable profit margins.

### 2. Prioritize High-Performing Markets

Markets and regions generating strong sales and profitability should be prioritized for future growth initiatives, while lower-performing regions should be analyzed to identify the causes of weaker performance.

### 3. Focus on Profitable Product Categories

Technology was the strongest contributor to overall profit. Maintaining strong performance in profitable categories while identifying opportunities to improve weaker categories could support long-term profitability.

### 4. Monitor Underperforming Regions

Regions with high sales but comparatively low profitability should be investigated further to identify potential issues related to discounts, product mix, or operating costs.

---

## 📈 Expected Impact

Implementing these recommendations could help the business:

- Improve overall profit margins by optimizing discount strategies.
- Focus growth efforts on high-performing markets and product categories.
- Identify underperforming regions and investigate potential causes.
- Support more data-driven decisions through continuous KPI monitoring.
